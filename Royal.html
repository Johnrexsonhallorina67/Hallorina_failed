Royal

About Royal

Royal is a popular carbonated soft drink known for its sweet and fruity orange flavor. Its bright citrus taste and refreshing fizz make it a great choice for enjoying with meals, celebrations, or whenever you want a refreshing drink.

Royal is a refreshing orange-flavored soft drink with a sweet, fruity taste and fizzy finish.

Fun Facts

  1. Royal is a popular orange-flavored soft drink in the Philippines.
  2. It has become a well-known Filipino soda brand and is commonly enjoyed with meals.
  3. Royal is recognized for its sweet, fruity orange flavor and refreshing fizz.
  4. It tastes especially refreshing when served chilled or over ice.
  5. Its bright orange branding matches its signature citrus flavor.
