About Mountain Dew

Mountain Dew is a refreshing carbonated soft drink known for its bold citrus flavor, lively fizz, and distinctive taste. Its bright and energetic character makes it a popular beverage for enjoying with meals, hanging out with friends, or simply cooling down on a warm day.

Mountain Dew is made with ingredients that create its signature bold and citrusy flavor. Its main ingredients typically include carbonated water, sweeteners, citric acid, natural and artificial flavors, and caffeine. These ingredients work together to give Mountain Dew its refreshing fizz, tangy taste, and distinctive flavor.

Fun Facts

  1. Mountain Dew was first created in the 1940s by beverage bottlers Barney and Ally Hartman.
  2. It is known for its bold citrus flavor and bright, fizzy taste.
  3. Mountain Dew traditionally contains caffeine, giving it a distinctive energetic image.
  4. Mountain Dew is sold in many countries around the world.
  5. The brand has been strongly associated with gaming, action sports, and youth culture.
