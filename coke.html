About Coke

Coke is a classic carbonated soft drink known for its refreshing taste and signature fizzy sensation. With its sweet and flavorful cola taste, it is a popular choice for enjoying with meals, celebrations , or simply when you want a refreshing drink.

Coca-Cola, commonly called Coke, is a carbonated soft drink created by John Stith Pemberton in 1886 in Atlanta, Georgia, USA. It became one of the world's most recognizable beverage brands.
