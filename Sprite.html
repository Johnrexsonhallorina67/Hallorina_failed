Sprite

About Sprite

Sprite is a refreshing carbonated soft drink with a crisp lemon-lime flavor. Its light, citrusy taste and fizzy bubbles make it a popular choice for cooling down and enjoying with meals, gatherings, or anytime you want a refreshing drink.

Sprite is a popular carbonated soft drink known for its crisp, refreshing lemon-lime flavor. It was introduced by The Coca-Cola Company in 1961 and is often enjoyed cold or with ice.

Quick Fact

  1. Brand: Sprite
  2. It is commonly served chilled or over ice.
  3. It is typically clear and colorless.
  4. Company: The Coca-Cola Company
  5. Known for: Crisp, citrusy, and refreshing taste
